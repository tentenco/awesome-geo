# Contributing to Awesome GEO

Thank you for helping build an open, current, and evidence-aware resource. Contributions of links, corrections, translations, research notes, tooling, and maintenance are welcome.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is made available under [CC0 1.0 Universal](LICENSE).

## Before opening a pull request

1. Search the repository and open issues for duplicates.
2. Prefer the original source over a summary or syndicated copy.
3. Verify that the URL works without requiring tracking parameters.
4. Identify the evidence class: official, peer-reviewed, preprint, independent study, vendor report, or community discussion.
5. Disclose your affiliation if you created, work for, advise, invest in, or receive compensation from the resource.
6. Use a neutral description and state important scope or methodology limits.

## What belongs here

- Official platform documentation and material product updates
- Reproducible or clearly scoped academic and industry research
- Useful open-source software, datasets, benchmarks, and protocols
- Established commercial tools with a distinct, verifiable capability
- High-signal practitioner guides and community discussions
- Corrections, archived links, translations, and accessibility improvements

## What does not belong here

- Affiliate or referral links
- Paid placement, link exchanges, or “featured tool” offers
- Undisclosed self-promotion
- Duplicate listicles, generic AI-written summaries, or copied descriptions
- Unsupported superlatives, fabricated statistics, or guaranteed ranking claims
- Fake reviews, astroturfing, automated community mentions, or spam tactics
- Resources whose primary purpose is unrelated to GEO, AEO, AI search, or their technical foundations

## Resource entry format

```markdown
- [Resource name](canonical URL) — Neutral description of what it provides.
  Evidence: official/peer-reviewed/preprint/independent/vendor/community.
  Published or materially updated: YYYY-MM-DD. Limitations: ...
```

Not every short list entry needs three lines, but research, annual updates, and contested claims should retain date, evidence class, and limitations.

## Pull request checklist

- [ ] The link is canonical, relevant, reachable, and free of tracking parameters.
- [ ] The description is factual, concise, and not copied from marketing text.
- [ ] Publication/update date and evidence class are included where relevant.
- [ ] Commercial and personal affiliations are disclosed in the PR.
- [ ] No existing content was removed without an explanation.
- [ ] Markdown links and relative paths have been checked.
- [ ] The “Last reviewed” date changed only for files actually reviewed.

## Editorial review

Maintainers evaluate relevance, source quality, novelty, neutrality, evidence transparency, maintenance risk, and community benefit. A popular resource may be declined if its claims cannot be checked. A commercial resource may be included when it fills a real gap and is described without promotion.

Decisions and substantial corrections should happen in public issues or pull requests whenever privacy and security allow. Maintainers may edit titles and descriptions for consistency.

## Translations

English is the canonical source for the detailed documentation. Translations should link to the canonical page and include a reviewed date. A translation does not need to follow an English sentence literally, but it must preserve meaning, evidence labels, caveats, and links.

If a translation falls materially behind, add a visible notice rather than silently presenting stale claims as current.

## Maintenance contributions

Broken-link repair is valuable. Prefer, in order: the new canonical URL; an official archive or replacement; a reputable web archive for historically important material; removal when the resource no longer has durable value.

For recurring research, update [docs/2026-landscape.md](docs/2026-landscape.md) with a dated source and add the change to the pull-request summary.

## Conduct and security

All participation follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Do not report private data or security vulnerabilities in a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
