# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and accepting constructive feedback, accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without explicit permission, undisclosed manipulation or astroturfing, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and privacy-aware.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

For confidential reports, use the repository's private [security advisory form](https://github.com/tentenco/awesome-geo/security/advisories/new) and state that the report concerns community conduct. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent possible. Maintainers involved in a report must recuse themselves from handling it.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider context, impact, intent, prior behavior, and the safety of affected community members. Responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary participation restriction, or permanent removal. Serious threats, harassment, or privacy violations may result in immediate removal.
