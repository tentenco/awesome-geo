# Governance

Awesome GEO is maintained in public for the benefit of practitioners, researchers, publishers, developers, and the wider open-web community.

## Principles

- **Community first:** no paid placement, affiliate ranking, or privileged vendor lane.
- **Evidence over hype:** identify source type, date, scope, and limitations.
- **Primary sources first:** link to platform documentation, papers, code, and data when available.
- **Transparent decisions:** discuss material additions, removals, and policy changes in issues or pull requests.
- **Portable knowledge:** use open formats and CC0 so others can reuse and improve the collection.
- **Safety and integrity:** reject manipulation, spam, fake consensus, harassment, and undisclosed conflicts.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone following the contribution and conduct policies may propose changes, review links, report errors, improve translations, or participate in discussion.

### Reviewers

Repeat contributors may be invited to triage issues and review pull requests. Reviewers check sources, dates, evidence labels, neutrality, and format but do not merge unless they are also maintainers.

### Maintainers

Maintainers merge changes, manage releases and automation, enforce community policies, handle sensitive reports, and keep the roadmap current. They must disclose relevant conflicts and should not approve their own commercial listing without another review when another maintainer is available.

## Decision process

Routine corrections and clearly qualifying resources may be merged after one maintainer review. Material policy changes, disputed removals, new top-level categories, and governance changes should remain open for community comment for at least seven days when practical.

The project seeks rough consensus. When consensus is not possible, maintainers decide using the published inclusion criteria and record the reason. Decisions may be revisited when new evidence appears.

## Becoming a maintainer

Candidates should have a sustained history of constructive contributions, careful source review, respectful communication, and disclosed conflicts. An existing maintainer nominates the candidate in a public issue; after at least seven days for feedback, current maintainers decide by simple majority.

## Inactivity and removal

Maintainers may step down at any time. A maintainer who is inactive for six months may be moved to emeritus status after a public notice. Access may be removed immediately for compromised accounts, serious security concerns, or severe Code of Conduct violations, with a written record when safety and privacy permit.

## Review cadence

- Weekly automation: external and relative link checking
- Monthly: triage issues and recent platform changes
- Quarterly: review official crawler controls, tool status, and high-volatility claims
- Annually: open a new dated landscape page and archive the prior year's snapshot

Dates indicate when content was reviewed, not a guarantee that every linked product remains unchanged.

## Commercial independence

The list accepts no payment for inclusion or placement. Sponsors may support general maintenance only if sponsorship does not influence editorial decisions and is publicly disclosed. Vendor employees may contribute under the same rules as everyone else.
